# Brand Guidelines (Concise)

## Voice & Tone

- Neutral, evidence-based, non-accusatory.
- Prefer: “According to a notice received on <date>…”
- Avoid: “Company X censors customers.”

## Logo Usage

- Clear space: at least 0.5× the logomark height around the lockup.
- Minimum size: logomark ≥ 18px on screen.
- Do not recolor the logomark; use primary or white on dark.
- Do not rotate, distort, add drop-shadows, or place on busy imagery.

## Colors

- Primary: Deep Atlantic Blue (#0B3B5F) — trust & research.
- Accent: Amber (#FFB200) — highlights & warnings.
- Teal (#1DA7A1), Slate (#334155), BG (#F6F8FA), Dark (#0F172A).

## Accessibility

- Aim for WCAG AA contrast. Test headings/body on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Reserve Amber for accents; never use as body text color on white.
